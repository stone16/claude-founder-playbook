# Founder Market Research

## When To Use

Use this skill when an idea needs competitor mapping, market sizing, category context, or trend analysis before an Idea gate decision. Do not use it to produce launch positioning, pricing pages, or investor narrative beyond the Idea-stage evidence needed here.

## Workflow

1. Define the market boundary in customer and job-to-be-done terms before sizing it.
2. Map competitors in tiers: direct substitutes, workflow-adjacent tools, manual workarounds, and status quo behavior.
3. Estimate TAM, SAM, and SOM with assumptions named separately from facts.
4. Identify trends that make the idea more or less urgent now, including regulatory, platform, budget, and behavior shifts.
5. Mark confidence levels and the next evidence needed from users or buyers.

## Artifacts Written

Write or update:

- `idea/competitive-landscape.md`
- `idea/market-sizing.md`
- `idea/trend-analysis.md`

Separate sourced facts from interpretation so later gate checks can trace what the founder actually knows.

Use `founder status` or `founder check` when validating the updated artifacts.
