# Customer Discovery Plan

## Target Interviewees

Three segments to cover in the next discovery round (target: 15-20 additional interviews):

- **Segment A: Production operators** (5-7 interviews). Engineers who have an agent running in production for 3+ months. Recruit via agent-focused Slack/Discord communities (LangChain Discord, AI Engineering Discord). Key to learn: what operational knowledge do they wish they had on day one?
- **Segment B: Evaluators** (5-7 interviews). Engineering managers at mid-size companies who are currently evaluating whether to adopt agents. Recruit via LinkedIn outreach and warm introductions. Key to learn: what information would change their build-vs-wait decision?
- **Segment C: Framework maintainers** (3-5 interviews). Contributors to major agent frameworks. Recruit via GitHub and X/Twitter. Key to learn: what comparisons do they consider fair, and what dimensions do they think practitioners undervalue?

## Questions

Core interview protocol (30 minutes):

1. Walk me through the last time you needed to make an agent architecture decision. What did you do? (Behavioral, not hypothetical)
2. What was the hardest piece of information to find? Where did you eventually find it?
3. What is something you believed 6 months ago about building agents that turned out to be wrong?
4. If you could hit a button and get one comprehensive resource about AI agents, what would it contain that does not exist today?
5. Have you ever contributed knowledge back to the community (blog post, talk, open source)? What motivated you? What prevented you from doing more?
6. (Pricing) If your team used a knowledge base like this, where would the budget come from — individual expense, team tools budget, or central engineering L&D? What approval would be needed?
7. (Distribution) How do you currently discover new AI engineering resources? Which channels (newsletters, X/Twitter, Slack communities, conference talks, direct colleague recommendations) are most likely to bring something like this to your attention?

## Recruiting Plan

- **Warm network**: 8-10 interviews via personal introductions and shared Slack/Discord communities.
- **Cold outreach**: Target authors of popular agent engineering blog posts and speakers at AI engineering conferences.
- **Incentive**: Share the synthesized findings with all participants before public release. Offer early access to the knowledge base.
- **Timeline**: Complete 15-20 interviews within 4 weeks. Synthesize and update artifacts by week 6.
